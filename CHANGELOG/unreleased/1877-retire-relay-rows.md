### Added

- **`aelf doctor --retire-relay-rows` retires beliefs stored from relay text as your words ([#1877](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1877)).** Before [#1875](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/pull/1875), ingest stored relay text that opened a user record, such as another session's message, as words you typed, with the `user_transcript` origin. The pass finds those rows with the rule #1875 cuts by. A stored row holds one sentence, so the pass takes a row that holds a relay opening tag alone or a host cross-session notice, when the row starts its turn. It reads a relay block's other rows from the DERIVED_FROM edge to your next turn, whose anchor keeps the earlier turn's text with its line breaks, up to 1,000 characters. A row that shares a timestamp with the row before it, as a bulk ingest's rows can, is never taken, and no row is taken because its group holds a relay row. A belief is retired only when every row that derived it is relay text. A locked belief is kept, as is one you promoted, confirmed, restored or kept in `aelf review`, and one another source corroborated. The report counts rows and beliefs by shape and lists belief ids without their text. It's a dry run unless you add `--apply`. `aelf doctor --restore-relay-rows --apply` restores every belief the pass retired, and `aelf restore <id>` restores one. A restored belief stays on a later run.
