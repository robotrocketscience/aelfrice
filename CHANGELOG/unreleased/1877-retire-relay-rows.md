### Added

- **`aelf doctor --retire-relay-rows` retires beliefs stored from relay text as your words ([#1877](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1877)).** Before [#1875](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/pull/1875), ingest stored relay text that opened a user record, such as another session's message, as words you typed, with the `user_transcript` origin. The pass finds those rows with the rule #1875 cuts by. A stored row holds one sentence, and a row starts its turn when the row before it has another session, timestamp, or source label. The pass takes a row that starts its turn and holds a relay opening tag alone, with the attributes the host writes on it, read outside quoted values. A bare tag such as `<agent-message>` doesn't count. It also takes the rows of a record that the DERIVED_FROM edge to your next turn shows opening with relay text, through the anchor that keeps the earlier turn's text with its line breaks, up to 1,000 characters. Every relay tag and notice line in the part #1875 cuts has to be the host's, each notice has to end at its template's fixed words, and the rows have to follow a row that starts its turn, in that row's group. A notice row is taken only this way. When that anchor shows a record #1875 keeps whole, such as one whose first line the sentence splitter dropped, the record's rows aren't taken, and no other anchor takes them. A relay record whose first row continues the group of the row before it, as in a bulk ingest that gives several records one timestamp, isn't taken. A belief is retired only when every row that derived it is relay text. A locked belief is kept, as is one you promoted, confirmed, restored or kept in `aelf review`, one another source corroborated, and one a federation peer owns. A tag with the host's attributes that you typed or pasted still reads as relay text. The report counts rows and beliefs by shape and lists belief ids without their text. It's a dry run unless you add `--apply`. `aelf doctor --restore-relay-rows --apply` restores each belief the pass retired that nothing has retired again since, and `aelf restore <id>` restores one. Undo leaves an audit trail: each restored belief keeps a `relay_rows:retire` feedback row of valence 0 and gains a `user_restored` row of valence 1, which keeps it on a later run, and its version counter and search index row change.
