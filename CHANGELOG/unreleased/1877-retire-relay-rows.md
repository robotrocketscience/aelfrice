### Added

- **`aelf doctor --retire-relay-rows` retires beliefs stored from relay text as your words ([#1877](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1877)).** Before [#1875](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/pull/1875), ingest stored relay text that opened a user record, such as another session's message, as words you typed, with the `user_transcript` origin. The pass finds those rows with the rule #1875 cuts by. A stored row holds one sentence, so the pass takes a row that starts its turn and holds either a relay opening tag alone, with the attributes the host writes on it, or a host cross-session notice with no word after its template's fixed words. A bare tag such as `<agent-message>` doesn't count. The pass reads a relay block's other rows from the DERIVED_FROM edge to your next turn, whose anchor keeps the earlier turn's text with its line breaks, up to 1,000 characters, and only when every relay tag and notice line in the part #1875 cuts is the host's. When that anchor shows a record that #1875 keeps whole, such as one whose first line the sentence splitter dropped, its first row isn't taken. A row that shares a timestamp with the row before it, as a bulk ingest's rows can, is never taken, and no row is taken because its group holds a relay row. A belief is retired only when every row that derived it is relay text. A locked belief is kept, as is one you promoted, confirmed, restored or kept in `aelf review`, and one another source corroborated. A tag with the host's attributes that you typed or pasted still reads as relay text. The report counts rows and beliefs by shape and lists belief ids without their text. It's a dry run unless you add `--apply`. `aelf doctor --restore-relay-rows --apply` restores each belief the pass retired that nothing has retired again since, and `aelf restore <id>` restores one. A restored belief stays on a later run.
