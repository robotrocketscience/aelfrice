### Changed

- **The H1 directive gate is a tripwire, so the bench tier is no longer red by design ([#1502](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1502)).** `test_directive_detection_gate` asserted `precision >= 0.80`, but that bar is the *reopening* criterion for work `docs/design/v2_enforcement.md` § H1 documents as deferred — so red was its correct, permanent, shipped state. It could never go green, and it held the whole bench-gate tier red at every release cut, in the tier that [#1477](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1477) made the project's only scheduled quality signal. A check that can never be green cannot be distinguished from a broken one, and it teaches a reader to skip the reds beside it. Inverted as `test_h1_reentry_bar_is_still_unmet`: green while the bar is unmet, and it fires when `detect_directive` clears both bars — the event that actually reopens [#374](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/374). The assertion is the conjunction of precision and recall, because that is how § H1 states the criterion, and the failure text says to reopen the issue rather than adjust the test. It also stamps the corpus digest, computed rather than pinned, because the corpus is now a union of v0.1 and v0.2 and the two score differently. Its companion `test_directive_corpus_defeats_a_first_token_baseline` goes **green** in the same change, once the v0.2 minimal-pair corpus is present: the one-token baseline clears the bar on 0 of 200 salted partitions against the union, where it cleared 196 of 200 against v0.1 alone. Latest measurement on the 510-row union: precision 0.665, recall 0.636 — H1 stays deferred, now on a number that means what § H1 intends.
