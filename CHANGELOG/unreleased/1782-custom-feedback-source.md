### Fixed

- **`aelf doctor --retire-scheduled-prompts --apply` keeps a belief you confirmed under a custom `--source` label ([#1782](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1782)).** The pass kept a confirmed belief only when the feedback row carried one of the default labels `user_confirmed`, `user`, or `user_restored`, so a belief you confirmed with `aelf confirm <id> --source me` was retired. A positive feedback row now keeps a belief unless its source is one that aelfrice writes on its own: a hook retrieval (`hook`), the deferred-feedback sweep (`retrieval_driven_feedback`), sentiment feedback (`sentiment_inferred`), `aelf clamp-ghosts` (`clamp_ghosts`), or the share of a feedback event that reaches a neighboring belief (a source that starts with `propagation:`). The pass reads only the label, so a confirmation under one of those labels, such as `--source hook`, doesn't keep a belief. To bring back a belief an earlier run retired, run `aelf restore <id>`.
