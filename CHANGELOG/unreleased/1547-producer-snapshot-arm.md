### Internal

- **`benchmarks/injection_budget_bytes.py` can now see the charge-vs-emit defect it was blind to ([#1547](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1547)).** **Nothing is fixed here.** The packer still charges a compressed form no renderer emits, and this change only makes the size of that gap measurable; #1547 AC4 requires the producer before any claim is made about the issue, and this is that producer. Two properties had made the defect invisible. The corpus held **zero** snapshot beliefs — `_synthetic_store` passed no `retention_class`, so every row landed on the `RETENTION_UNKNOWN` default, which `compress_for_retrieval` maps to `verbatim`, and every charge equalled its emission by construction. And no lane composed: seven lanes each rendered one block, while the live 66,165-character row that prompted the issue came from the **first-prompt envelope**, where `hook._build_session_start_subblock` and the per-turn pack are rendered into one `<aelfrice-memory>` block by `hook._format_hits_with_session_start`. There is now a `first_prompt` lane that renders exactly that composition, registered in `_RENDERERS` and `LANES` like any other, and a snapshot arm built from a `SNAPSHOT_EVERY` stride beside the existing `LOCK_EVERY` and `SPECULATIVE_EVERY` ones. `corpus_shape` reads `retention_class` back off the store, reporting `snapshot` and `snapshot_unlocked` separately because a *locked* snapshot renders verbatim — locks override retention class — so the published counts are measured rather than taken from the generator's arithmetic. On the arm's corpus that reads 43 and 42 of 300; on the control corpus it reads 0 and 0, which is the old blindness now printed rather than assumed. `corpus_shape` reports a `sentence_headline` count beside those, because the retention class is only half of what the arm's corpus adds: the count is the beliefs carrying a sentence boundary at or before `compression.MAX_HEADLINE_CHARS`, taken from `compression._first_sentence_end_outside_fence` rather than from a second copy of its rule, and it reads **300** of 300 on the prose and snapshot corpora against **0** on the control. All three shapes are now read at the top of the arm's grid rather than at its foot: at 92 content characters no belief closes a sentence, so a shape read there could confirm the class was written but never the text change.

  **Charged against emitted, one belief, by retention class.** One constraint had to be met before the arm measured anything, and the old generator never met it: a sentence boundary — a `. ` or `.\n` outside a code fence — ending at or before `compression.MAX_HEADLINE_CHARS` (240). That boundary is the only thing that takes `compression._headline` down its first-sentence branch. The guard that returns the content unchanged is conjunctive — no boundary **and** no longer than the cap — so length on its own is neither necessary nor sufficient, and the table below shows it: at 150 and 200 content characters, both under the cap, a `snapshot` belief reports strategy `headline`, charged 44 tokens, ratio 1.2x and 1.4x. Past the cap with no boundary the function hard-truncates instead, which is a third code path with a third byte count. The generator now closes a sentence every `SENTENCE_CHARS` (120) characters, and `LENGTH_GRID` runs `(40, 92, 150, 200, 300, 1000, 5950, 7170, 18600)` — the first two below `SENTENCE_CHARS` and therefore the arm's inert control, 200 at `hook_search_tool.PER_LINE_CHAR_CAP`, 300 the old top of the grid, then p90 of live per-turn `<belief>` element size, the 5,950 characters that separate the two arms' `<core>` accountings (below), p99, and the 18,600 characters #1547 measured at.

  **This disagrees with the issue's prior, and the producer wins.** #1547 reads 150.4x and 388.6x at 18,600 characters; this measures 106.0x and 186.5x. The emitted side agrees exactly — 4,663 tokens on both — and the whole of the difference is on the charged side, where `retrieval._render_wrapper_tokens` adds the 13-token `<belief>` element to every compressed render. That is [#1526](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1526)'s correction, and it postdates the figure the issue quotes; net of it the two tables agree cell for cell, 31 against 31 and 12 against 12. The element is emitted whether or not the content was shortened, so it belongs in the charge, and the smaller ratio is the one to carry. The ratio is still a property of the corpus as much as of the defect — it is set by where the first sentence ends — so the charged and emitted token columns are published beside it and the multiplier is never quoted alone.

  **Three corpora, so the class effect is attributable.** The arm is measured against a middle corpus that adds the sentence boundaries and nothing else, because a two-corpus comparison would confound the retention class with the text change needed to make it fire. `control → prose` is that text change, and it is close to nil: on `ups` it is byte-identical at 92, 1,000, 7,170 and 18,600 content characters and 6,427 → 6,763 bytes at 300; on `first_prompt` the same, plus one byte at 1,000 and two at 7,170 from the `<core>` section packed above the hits. `prose → snapshot` is the class. On `ups` at 18,600 characters the prose pack admits **no non-locked belief at all** and ends on the candidate pool — one verbatim belief costs 4,663 tokens against `DEFAULT_HOOK_TOKEN_BUDGET = 1500`, so all that survives is the six locks [#379](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/379) exempts. The snapshot pack ends on `token_budget`: the budget binds, and binds *later*, admitting 22 beliefs charged 723 tokens whose emitted text is **74,616 tokens**, 103.2x. A budget reporting that it is doing its job is the defect here, not the absence of one. The composed lane reads the same shape one envelope up: at 18,600 characters `first_prompt` emits **113,434** bytes on the prose corpus, all of it the exempt sections, against **412,121** bytes and **22** admitted beliefs on the snapshot corpus. Both budgets are varied on all three sides, so every cell still names the cap that ended it, which is the property [#1546](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1546) requires of any new measurement.

  **What the composed lane measures, and the fifth cost function it needed.** At the corpus median of 92 content characters the first-prompt envelope goes 19,999 → 13,365 bytes, **-33.2%**, against the per-turn lane's 9,259 → 6,763. The composed change is larger than the per-turn change because the envelope also carries `<core>`, whose accounting #1526 changed as well — and the first revision of this lane did not measure that. `_legacy_accounting` rebound four names; `_build_session_start_subblock` packs `<core>` through `hook._core_belief_cost`, a fifth, and unlike the standalone `core` lane it takes no `cost_fn` to pass a legacy body into. Every composed before cell was therefore a hybrid of pre-#1526 retrieval cost and post-#1526 `<core>` cost, reading 15,861 bytes where a consistent before arm reads 19,999 and publishing -15.7% for a -33.2% change. The rebind set is now a table, the `core` lane's explicit plumbing is gone in favour of it, and `tests/test_render_cost_1526.py` scans `src/aelfrice` for the #1526 cost functions and fails when one is in neither the rebind table nor the named-exception table.

  **The AC2 dedupe now has a producer too.** The envelope dedupe that shipped in this issue could not be measured by any lane here, because it only fires where a belief appears in both halves of a composed envelope. On the `first_prompt` lane at 92 content characters, 13 of 41 hits are already rendered in a 6,813-character session-start sub-block and the envelope comes out 13,577 → 13,365 bytes, **-1.6%**. The lane's `<recent-work>` section is built on a non-git cwd, and its size is read back out of the sub-block the lane rendered rather than from a second call to the builder: a separate probe agrees with the lane only while both resolve the same cwd, so it constrained nothing. Read off the block it measures 0 characters, and pointing the lane at a git cwd moves both that figure and every composed byte count together.

  **No published #1526 figure moved.** The generator's word-list re-join was quadratic and was 9.8 of the 12.5 seconds one 18,600-character store took to build, which is the mechanical reason the grid could not previously reach the lengths the defect lives at; it now accumulates a running length instead and emits byte-identical content, checked by `scripts/check_derived_figures.py --mode all`, which re-runs this producer and diffs every marker naming it against the value published beside it. The extended grid did surface one new measurement: above `DEFAULT_SESSION_START_CORE_TOKEN_BUDGET` a single `<core>` line no longer fits, so at 5,950 content characters and above `_pack_core_candidates` skips all 300 candidates and the section emits **0 bytes**. Those zeros are measured values, not suppressed cells, and `test_the_producer_names_which_budget_ended_every_pack` admits them — and no others — by re-deriving the condition that produces them: a zero cell must come from `<core>`, at a length where one `<core>` line costs more than `DEFAULT_SESSION_START_CORE_TOKEN_BUDGET` in the currency the emitting arm packs under. Every other lane and length reaches that same branch and fails it, which is where the floor lives. `test_the_core_section_empties_once_one_belief_exceeds_its_budget` pins the same threshold independently. The producer run is also now pinned against the ambient environment: its fixture clears every `AELFRICE_` variable, because a dozen resolvers it reaches — `AELFRICE_TYPE_AWARE_COMPRESSION` among them — read one before anything else, and a value exported in a shell must not move a published figure.

  **Three of the guards over this producer could not fail.** An adversarial re-review of the tests above found each one, and the shape is the same in all three: an assertion written against the thing that produced it. `_recent_work_chars_in` replaced by `return 0` left the suite green, *including* the cwd mutation the reader exists to catch — a reader that always answers zero cannot see the lane move, so the one assertion on it held either way. The snapshot arm's lane set was checked with `set(arm) == set(SNAPSHOT_ARM_LANES)`, comparing the produced dict against the constant that produced it, so dropping `first_prompt` from the constant dropped the composed envelope out of the arm and stayed green. And the middle corpus had no assertion that it differed from the control at all: generated with its sentence boundaries left off it is byte-identical to the control at every length, and every assertion written against it stays true while it attributes nothing. Each now carries a check that must go the other way — the reader is asserted to find the section in a block that carries one, the lanes are named literally, and the middle corpus must differ from the control both in `sentence_headline` and in rendered bytes at some length in the grid. All three are mutation-proved against the exact mutation that exposed them.

  **Two more, including one the repair above introduced.** A second adversarial pass found that the lane-set repair had kept its unfalsifiable half, and that one measured zero had been paid for with a floor across the whole grid. The set equality `set(arm) == set(SNAPSHOT_ARM_LANES)` was kept beside the literal lane names to catch "a produced key no constant declares", which it cannot do: `snapshot_arm` builds its dict by iterating that constant and nothing mutates it afterwards, so the equality is an invariant of the function under every mutation of every file. It is replaced by `set(arm) <= set(LANES)`, which the arm is not generated from and which fails when the arm measures a lane no #1526 curve covers. Separately, the per-cell floor had gone from `> 0` to `>= 0` across all eight lanes and every grid length to admit the single `<core>` cell measured to be empty, so any lane could emit nothing at any length with the suite green: an oversize guard on `_render_session_start` turns that lane's published **112,456**-byte cell at 18,600 content characters into a zero, and under the relaxed floor and the per-lane floor beside it the file stayed green. The floor is `> 0` again, with the one zero admitted by re-deriving the condition that empties it, and the same mutation now reds `test_the_producer_names_which_budget_ended_every_pack` on `assert 'session_start' == 'core'`. Both are mutation-proved, each against the mutation that exposed it and against a control that shows the replaced assertion passing on it.

  **And two more inside that repair.** A third adversarial pass read the exemption the floor was traded for and found both of its assertions inert. The exemption branched on the packing arm's accounting, and the claim that a zero is read in the currency of the arm that packed it does not hold up: the two currencies never separate on this grid.   **The last one needed a grid length, not an assertion.** A fourth pass read the `min(legacy, shipped) > budget` repair above and found it inert for the same reason the branch it replaced was: `min` of that pair is identically the pre-#1526 half, because `max(1, len(content) // 4)` is strictly below the shipped charge on the rendered line at every content length — 0 exceptions over 1..20,000 step 37 — and replacing the `min` with either operand alone left the file green.
