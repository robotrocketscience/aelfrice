### Fixed

- **A cadence-resume recap no longer displaces the prompt's own retrieval hits ([#1564](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1564)).** `_ceiling_drop_order` bucketed the block ceiling's droppable elements by "not inside `<core>` and not inside `<recent-work>`" and called the result the per-turn hits. That is a negative test, and it silently accepted a third kind of element: the [#871](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/871) `<cadence-resume>` recap, which `user_prompt_submit` prepends ahead of `<session-start>`. Because the recap is *prepended* it landed at the head of that bucket and `reversed(lane)` shed it last, so the prompt's own hits were deleted to keep a synthesis of an earlier session — the exact inversion of what the shed order exists to prevent. A second mechanism compounded it: `_format_hits_with_session_start` read `_ids_rendered_verbatim_in` off the sub-block *after* the recap had been concatenated onto the front of it, so every id the recap rendered collapsed the prompt's own hit to a `seen` pointer, and `enforce_block_ceiling` deletes a pointer with the element it names. Measured on a real P1 resume cache against a 40-lock / 20-core / 20-hit store at the shipped 6,000-token ceiling: **6 prompt-matched beliefs reached the model without a recap and 0 with one**. The operator ruled on 2026-09-17 that **the recap sheds first, and whole, and never suppresses a hit**, and this is that ruling: a fourth lane ahead of `<core>`, classified positively by the recap's own wrapper rather than by what it is not; `_recap_shed`, which removes the whole `<cadence-resume>` span in one cut so no wrapper is ever left holding a fragment of its beliefs; and `_session_start_dedupe_ids`, which cuts the recap's span out before the dedupe scan so only the sub-block's own `<locked>` and `<core>` elements can point at a hit. The same fixture now reaches **6 of 6 either way**, at a cost of **444 estimated tokens** on the untrimmed envelope — the part of [#1547](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1547)'s dedupe that fell on recap-carried ids, given back. **Taking the recap out of the dedupe changes what "dropped" means, and the ceiling now says so.** Since the recap no longer suppresses anything, every belief it carries is rendered a second time below it — in `<locked>`, in `<core>` or as the prompt's own hit — and cutting the recap's span does not take that copy with it. Reporting the span's ids wholesale therefore named beliefs the model was shown, and `BlockCeilingOutcome.dropped_ids` is read by four accounting surfaces as the opposite claim: `user_prompt_submit` filters `emitted_hits` by it, and that list is what reaches the `feedback_history` exposure rows, the audit row `aelf tail` prints, the [#740](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/740) dedup ring, and the [#1382](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1382) ledger. On the same fixture that put **5 rendered prompt hits and 40 rendered user locks into a list every one of those surfaces reads as "never shown"**, and took the audit row from 37 injected beliefs to 1 beside a payload carrying 65. `enforce_block_ceiling` now reports an id from a whole-recap shed only when nothing outside the recap renders it, so the count is beliefs the block lost every render of rather than elements one cut removed — which is what the four call sites were already written against. The stderr note is derived from that count and reads `1` where the span was 65; `scripts/measure_block_ceiling.py --resume-drop` is what reports the span. A `lock="user"` element inside a recap keeps the wrapper, because the [#379](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/379) always-injected contract outranks the whole-shed rule; no shipped render produces one today, since `context_rebuilder` spells a lock `locked="true"`, and the branch is tested rather than assumed. **A fourth change was asked for, implemented, and refuted by the suite.** An adversarial read of the call site held that `_verbatim_ids` must take the renderer's augmented set — `already_rendered` unioned with the sub-block's own ids — rather than the caller's `read_rendered(session_id)`, on the grounds that [#1382](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1382) AC4 asks for one derivation of the manifest-vs-verbatim predicate and this is two. The two sets differ on exactly the hits the sub-block rendered verbatim above the pack, and those are the hits whose text *is* in the context window, because the sub-block put it there: recording them is the true claim and the union drops it. Under the change, `test_ups_seen_pointer_on_turn_two_names_a_belief_turn_one_rendered` went red with zero `seen` pointers on turn two — #1382 stops working across turns. The change is reverted, the call site now says why the caller's set is the right one, and `test_the_ledger_keeps_a_belief_the_sub_block_rendered` states the property positively so the next reader does not repeat it. `tests/test_hook_ceiling_cadence_resume_1560.py` pinned all four of the old claims and is renamed to `tests/test_hook_recap_shed_order_1564.py` and rewritten against the ruling, with eight tests and one mutation per claim: dropping the recap lane fails the ordering claim, skipping the whole-shed branch brings back a wrapper holding a fragment, restoring `_ids_rendered_verbatim_in` at the render site fails both hit claims, handing `_verbatim_ids` the augmented set fails the ledger claim, removing the `lock="user"` fallback fails the #379 claim, and dropping the test that keeps a still-rendered id out of `dropped_ids` fails the accounting claim on both of its arms — the invariant on a hand-built body, and the fired hook's audit row, which goes back to naming 1 injected belief beside 5 prompt hits it rendered. Out of scope by ruling: bounds across blocks ([#1560](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1560)), whether cadence should default on, and the `_LOCKED_ATTR` / `locked="true"` spelling mismatch that keeps the #379 contract from holding on the recap path. That last one is deferred, not closed, and it is not free: a user lock the recap carries now sheds first rather than last, where before it survived on this fixture. It is bounded by `<locked>`, which renders the same locks uncapped in the same envelope, so the text still reaches the model and the belief stays out of `dropped_ids` — with one exception, recorded at the `n_locked` call site in `hook.py`: a [#1558](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1558) reference lock, whose `<locked>` render is a `ref` manifest line rather than an element, so the recap's bounded-topic element is the only one there is to shed. The manifest line survives and still names it.
