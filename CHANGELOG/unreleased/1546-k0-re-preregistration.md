### Documentation

- **K0 is re-registered, and its verdict re-derived under the replacement ([#1546](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1546)).** The operator voided K0's original pre-registration because it described the instrument wrongly in four places: it called the cluster structure inert when `clustering.pack_with_clusters` runs on every query of every measured cell, treated probe containment as a theorem when it holds only because the census's stores carry no edges, inherited a containment mutation guard with no power on the `ups`, `retrieval_default`, and `rebuilder` lanes, and attributed the absent BFS lane to the missing edges rather than to the default-off `[retrieval] bfs_enabled` flag. `docs/design/budget_discriminability_k0_preregistration.md` replaces it. It carries every decision criterion forward unchanged — the statistic, the population rule, the grey band, the required N, the cut conditions, the forbidden fallbacks, and the finding wording — and adds only the disclosed limits and a set of kill criteria split by whether these corpora can supply the input that trips them, so a criterion that cannot fire is named as a limit instead of listed as a guard. `docs/design/budget_discriminability_decision_rule.md` is retained, banner-marked as voided, and unamended below the banner. No published figure moved: all 22 keys of `--emit-figures` compare equal, `--check` is byte-identical across two runs, and `tests/test_budget_census.py` passes.
