### Fixed

- **A live test asserted general budget monotonicity on a fixture that could not disprove it ([#1574](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1574)).** `tests/test_retrieval_token_budget.py::test_tighter_budget_returns_no_more_results_than_looser` claimed "as budget shrinks, output length is non-increasing". Every belief in its fixture is `body[:content_len]`, so all of them cost the same; under equal prices the greedy fill is a prefix and cannot evict, which made the assertion unfailable — the same shape that hid the defect in the census fixture. The property is false in general: with a dear top-ranked belief and two cheap ones, raising the budget from 60 to 61 takes the output from two hits to one. The test is now scoped to its fixture, asserts that the fixture really is equal-priced, and is paired with an arm that pins the eviction on unequal prices.
