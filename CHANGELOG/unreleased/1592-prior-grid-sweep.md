### Added

- **`benchmarks/posterior_prior_grid_sweep.py` measures how much real evidence has reached belief posteriors, across every store on a host ([#1592](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1592)).** #1592 reported that all 19,875 beliefs in one store sit on their insertion prior, so `posterior_weight` reranks by `(belief_type, source)` rather than by observed confidence. That store reproduces — 7 distinct pairs, none off the prior grid — and the sweep confirms the conclusion while correcting its mechanism. Sitting on a prior does **not** mean no evidence arrived: `apply_feedback(update_posterior=False)` writes the audit row and skips the bump, which since [#1086](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1086) is the default for retrieval exposure, and `clamp_ghosts` writes a *lower* alpha that can push a moved belief back onto a prior. Across 26 stores and 172,089 beliefs, 6,220 sit on a prior while carrying a real non-zero-valence feedback event — 283 of them on this repo's own store, which took 734 such events and moved nothing. Of the 9,413 beliefs that are off the grid, 2,392 are dedupe mass (the canonical row of a collapsed duplicate group holds the *sum* of the group's alphas and betas, so `n` copies of one prior land at `n * prior` with the posterior mean exactly unchanged, and retrieval blends `log(posterior_mean)`), and **6,761 of the remaining 7,021 — 96.3% — have their earliest feedback event before the 2026-07-04 commit that made retrieval exposure audit-only.** They moved under a rule the tree removed as a defect. Fewer than 300 beliefs on the entire host carry a posterior moved by the feedback path as it ships today. The producer classifies against the complete set of insertion priors, built by calling `get_source_adjusted_prior` over the real `TYPE_PRIORS` so the grid cannot drift from the shipped resolver, and identifies the pair #1592 left unexplained: `(0.3, 1.0)` is `wonder/lifecycle.py`'s speculative ingest default, another prior rather than an update. Each store is byte-copied with its write-ahead log and opened `read_only=True`, since a read-write open runs schema DDL and migrations, which is a write.
