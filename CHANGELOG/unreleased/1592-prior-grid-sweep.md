### Added

- **`benchmarks/posterior_prior_grid_sweep.py` measures how many beliefs carry a moved posterior, across every store on a host ([#1592](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1592)).** #1592 reported that all 19,875 beliefs in one store sit on their insertion prior, so `posterior_weight` reranks by `(belief_type, source)` rather than by observed confidence. That store reproduces exactly — 19,876 beliefs, 7 distinct pairs, none off the prior grid — but the finding does not generalize: across 26 stores and 172,055 beliefs, 9,413 beliefs (5.47%) sit off the grid, and 13 of the 21 non-empty stores carry at least one. The producer classifies each `(alpha, beta)` against the complete set of insertion priors, built by calling `get_source_adjusted_prior` over the real `TYPE_PRIORS` rather than by transcribing its arithmetic, so the grid cannot drift from the shipped resolver and silently reclassify ordinary insertions as evidence. It also splits the off-grid population by whether the posterior *mean* moved, because `MemoryStore` dedupe collapses duplicates by summing their alphas and betas onto the canonical row: `n` copies of one prior land at `n * prior`, off the grid and carrying `n` times the mass, with the mean exactly unchanged. Retrieval blends `log(posterior_mean)`, so those 2,448 beliefs score precisely as they did before they moved, and only the remaining 6,965 (4.05%) are evidence that the feedback loop ever ran. The issue's one unexplained pair, `(0.3, 1.0)`, is identified as the wonder-ingest speculative default — another insertion prior, not an update. Each store is byte-copied with its write-ahead log before being opened `read_only=True`, since a read-write open runs schema DDL and migrations, which is a write.
