### Added

- **Automatic phantom promotion on your own evidence, off by default ([#1650](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1650)).** This is the first three parts of the plan in `docs/design/feature-supports-writer.md`:
  - **Speaker on corroborations:** `belief_corroborations` gains a nullable `speaker` column. Transcript ingest fills it from the turn's role; every other source, and every earlier row, leaves it empty. A later write from a user turn upgrades an existing row to `user`.
  - **`evidence_promoted` origin:** a phantom promoted automatically gets this origin. It ranks with `user_transcript` in retrieval and in contradiction precedence, below `user_validated`, and renders with the inferred tier. `aelf demote` returns it to `speculative`, and the rule never promotes that phantom again.
  - **`[phantom_promotion] auto_promote` / `AELFRICE_PHANTOM_AUTO_PROMOTE`:** when it's on, a phantom is promoted once it has at least 3 supports from turns you typed, across at least 2 sessions. Each support must be recorded after the phantom was created and come from a session other than the one that created it. The phantom also needs no CONTRADICTS edge and no negative feedback. A support is a complete restatement: ingest stores what you type one sentence at a time, and a phantom is usually a paragraph, so a session counts only when you typed every sentence of the phantom that ingest would store as yours, each matched by exact text. A phantom with anything outside those sentences other than whitespace and a short list of ASCII structure marks, such as list markers and sentence punctuation, is never promoted automatically, because promotion keeps its whole text. Each turn promotes up to five phantoms and adds a notice with the undo command.

  The spec's first version assumed a restatement corroborates the phantom directly. It doesn't, because a phantom's identity comes from wonder's inputs rather than its text, and the spec now says so. SUPPORTS edges from reworded restatements will count once their writer exists. Nothing changes until you turn the switch on.
