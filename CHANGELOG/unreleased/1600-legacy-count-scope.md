### Fixed

- **The prior-grid sweep counted pre-#1086 legacy moves over a different population than the share it printed ([#1600](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1600)).** `benchmarks/posterior_prior_grid_sweep.py` counted a belief as a legacy move if it was off the insertion-prior grid and its earliest non-zero-valence feedback event predated the 2026-07-04 exposure change — but printed that count as a share of the *genuinely-moved* beliefs, which is `off_prior - mean_preserving` and excludes dedupe mass. A collapsed duplicate group whose canonical row carries an old feedback event therefore landed in the numerator and not the denominator, so the printed share was inflated and could in principle exceed 100%; a two-belief fixture with one of each makes it print 200.0%. `_feedback_split` now applies the same `_matches_prior_multiple` test `probe` already uses, so the numerator is scoped to the population the share reports on, and the output line names that population. The classifier is unchanged — [#1598](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1598) established the uniform/moved split is right on real data. On the measured host the correction moves the count by one, from 6,761 to 6,760: exactly one dedupe-mass belief carries a pre-2026-07-04 event, at `(5.0, 5.0)`, five copies of `derivation.py`'s git triple-extraction prior. The published 96.3% share is unchanged (6,760/7,021 = 96.28%).
