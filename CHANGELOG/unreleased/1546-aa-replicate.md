### Added

- **K3: the budget census's noise floor now carries a measured A/A term, and it is 0.0pp ([#1546](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1546)).** `docs/design/budget_discriminability_k0_preregistration.md` builds the floor as `NF = max(1.96 * sqrt(0.25 / N), 9.5pp, A/A_band)` and records that the third term was missing, so the band covered grader disagreement and binomial sampling noise and no instrument noise at all. `scripts/budget_discriminability_aa_replicate.py` supplies it, and `grey_band()` now takes it: `AA_BAND_PP` is a pinned literal with `AA_BAND_MEASURED` beside it, because a zero that *was measured* and a term that *does not exist* were previously the same number, and the flag is what tells them apart. A test compares the constant against the producer's own figure, so the two cannot drift. The census is deterministic — `--check` hashes two reports on every run — so a replicate that re-ran the same inputs would measure zero by construction; the perturbation is instead belief **insertion order**, re-ingested under a seeded permutation that holds the corpus, the gold sets, the grid, the lane set, and every belief's content fixed and moves only the rowid SQLite assigns. The producer reuses `budget_discriminability_census.report(queries=...)` rather than reimplementing EC, so the lanes, grid, cost functions, exclusion rules, block-identity check, and containment check are the census's own. Measured over 9 replicates at base seed 1546: `aa_band_pp` 0.0, on all six lane cells and all twelve lane-by-corpus cells, with N holding at 7 and 0 bound violations. `NF` is unchanged at 37.0398pp, because the binomial term dominates at N=7 — the census's `--check` hash moves only because `grey_band_has_aa_term` flips from `false` to `true`. The zero is earned rather than asserted: a positive control that makes retrieval read the rowid drives the band to 100.0pp and 210 of 2070 order-sensitive grid arms at the same replicate count, and a sweep of every lane, query, and grid cell finds 0 of 2070 whose retrieved list moves under permutation — so the band is not zero because movements cancelled, the inputs to EC are invariant. Two limits are published beside the figure rather than left to a reader: the term is estimated where **no cap binds on any cell**, a regime in which EC is 0 by construction and cannot move, and the temporal spine — the one lane that orders by rowid — never runs on an edgeless corpus.
