### Fixed

- **The commit hook reads only a lowercase relation on one line ([#1874](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1874)).** The hook ignored case in the verb, so an uppercase edge-type name used as a noun, such as "the SUPERSEDES writer", wrote a false edge. Its matches also crossed line breaks, so a commit's subject and body could join into one belief. The verb now has to match in lowercase, so "SUPERSEDES" and "Supersedes" no longer count. Each match stays on one line of the message. A phrase that wraps to the next line is cut at the line break, and a relation is lost when the break touches the verb or leaves only a word such as "the" on one side. The words stored from a match can still fail to form a complete phrase. Beliefs and edges already in your store don't change: their log rows hold the stored phrases, so they replay as they were written. The comment in the `post-commit` block now says the hook stores the words on each side of a phrase, not the commit's message, and `aelf setup` updates it the next time it runs in a repository. Retrieval's query building and the entity index don't change.
