### Fixed

- **The calibration gate no longer calls an aggregation collision "inert" ([#1584](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1584)).** `test_disabling_the_blend_changes_the_metrics` compared ROC-AUC and Spearman ρ between the shipped `posterior_weight` and `0.0`. Both are statistics of the pooled `(score, label)` observations as a multiset, so they agree whenever per-query rank moves cancel across the corpus — and on the bundled 7-fixture corpus `posterior_weight = 1.5` does exactly that, matching the `0.0` arm to every digit of both metrics while retrieving a different ranking on three of seven queries. The assertion was therefore a false positive waiting to fire: moving the shipped default into that band reds it with a message reading "the rerank is inert on this corpus" at a weight where the ranking demonstrably changed. The guard now compares **rankings**, which is the property it is named for, and claims inertness only where inertness holds. `CalibrationReport` gained a `rankings` field carrying each query's ranked candidate ids, recorded by the same loop that builds the observations so the two can never describe different runs; it is read by name everywhere, so `aelf eval --json` and the pinned baselines are unchanged.
