### Performance

- **The BM25 sidecar is warmed by a detached child at `SessionStart`, so the session-first full rebuild is no longer paid on the user's first prompt ([#1513](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1513)).** `benchmarks/sidecar_rebuild_rate.py` now buckets scored fires by position within the session, and the two populations do not resemble each other. Re-derived 2026-08-26 over 19 audit logs / 3,802 UPS fires / 123 scored, the session-FIRST bucket runs `full_rebuild 6, fresh 17, incremental 3` for **6/26 = 23.1%**, against `full_rebuild 1, fresh 87, incremental 9` for **1/97 = 1.0%** on every later fire — a 23x separation, on the fire where a user is least willing to wait. The issue's original n=39 sample read 5/13 = 38.5% against 0/26 = 0.0%; the sign held and the magnitude moved, exactly as its own caution said it would. Rebuild cost in that sample ranged 347 ms to 1825 ms.

  **Pre-registered target, committed before any post-fix measurement:** the session-FIRST `full_rebuild` rate falls to **≤ 10%** (from the 23.1% re-derived baseline), the LATER bucket does not rise above its 1.0% baseline, and the rate is read only once at least 26 sessions carry a scored fire — the size of the baseline sample. The fix cannot reach 0%: a user who types immediately, or a sibling session that ingests between the warm and the first prompt, still meets a stale index.

  The warm is a **detached child process** spawned from the SessionStart hook, chosen against the failure mode rather than the happy path. *A thread the hook does not join* was rejected: the hook is a short-lived process, so a non-daemon thread keeps the interpreter alive until the build finishes and the host waits on the hook — that moves the latency into `SessionStart` instead of removing it, which the issue's AC2 names explicitly; a daemon thread inverts the failure and is killed mid-build, writing nothing. *Warming on a later fire* was rejected on the measurement: the later bucket is already 1.0%, so a warm that runs on the second fire arrives after the only fire that pays. The parent pays one `Popen` and nothing else — measured **median 1.62 ms, max 3.17 ms** over 20 spawns at one-per-session cadence, against the 347–1825 ms tail it displaces — because every decision that costs anything (is the L1 lane on, is the sidecar fresh, what tokenisation parameters apply) is made inside the child.

  Both halves are fail-soft and neither can change what the hook emits: a spawn that raises returns False, a warm that raises returns None, and `SessionStart`'s stdout, stderr and return code are pinned byte-identical between a suppressed warm and one that explodes. The child builds through the *same* helper and resolvers the L1 lane uses, which is load-bearing rather than tidy — a sidecar written under different tokenisation parameters is rejected as describing different documents, so a warm that resolved its own would pay the whole build and leave the next fire rebuilding anyway. `AELF_NO_SIDECAR_WARM=1` suppresses the spawn.
