### Documentation

- **`aelf wonder` with no topic lists related beliefs and merges nothing ([#389](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/389)).** The 1.7.0 entry said that `aelf wonder` walks the speculative-belief frontier to surface phantoms that "the consolidation pipeline has not yet promoted", and the 3.0.0 entry said that the no-topic form "continues to run graph-walk consolidation". The no-topic pass starts from a seed belief, lists the beliefs that a breadth-first walk reaches from it, and merges nothing. Nothing hands that list to a merge step. `aelf doctor --consolidate` is a read-only report, and the one-time content-hash migration, which does collapse duplicate beliefs, runs when the store opens and doesn't read the wonder list. The `/aelf:wonder` slash command, `aelf wonder --help`, `docs/user/COMMANDS.md`, and `docs/user/SLASH_COMMANDS.md` now describe the pass as a list of related beliefs.
