### Removed

- **`hook.DEFAULT_SESSION_START_TOKEN_BUDGET` and the `token_budget` parameter of `hook.session_start()` are deleted; the SessionStart block is bounded by the lock count ([#1546](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1546)).** The constant could not bind on the lane it governed, at any value. `session_start` retrieves through `_retrieve_baseline_with_block`, which calls `retrieve()` with an **empty query**, and in `retrieve_with_tiers` every relevance lane — L2.5, L1, HRR expansion, the temporal spine and the BFS hop — is gated on `query.strip()`. Only L0 contributes, and L0 is appended unconditionally and never trimmed ([#379](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/379)), so the pack loop a budget drives is never entered. Reproduced before the deletion on a 300-lock store of 150-character beliefs: 300 hits and 61,144 rendered bytes at `token_budget=1`, at 1500 and at 100000 alike. **The knob the deletion exposes is the TOML key, not the environment variable.** `resolve_token_budget`'s precedence is environment variable, then the explicit keyword argument, then `[retrieval] token_budget` in `.aelfrice.toml`, then the default, so `AELFRICE_RETRIEVAL_TOKEN_BUDGET` already outranked the budget this caller passed and already reached the lane: `resolve_token_budget(1500, start=<dir>)` returns 111 with that variable set to `111`. What is new is the TOML key, which the explicit argument used to shadow — with `[retrieval] token_budget = 77` on disk, the same call returns 1500 in the pre-deletion shape (`explicit=1500`) and 77 in the post-deletion one (`explicit=None`). Both are inert here, because the pack loop is never entered: the guard below drives the production call under four environment values, under `[retrieval] token_budget = 1`, and under neither, and gets the same 10 locks and the same block length from all six arms. The bound that remains is the one [#379](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/379) states: every locked belief is injected, and the lock count is the operator's knob.

  The guard is kept and re-aimed. `tests/test_render_cost_1526.py::test_session_start_lane_never_trims_its_l0_pool` (renamed from `test_session_start_budget_does_not_bind_on_its_own_lane`) asserts the [#379](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/379) contract directly rather than asserting a now-deleted constant: it drives the real `_retrieve_baseline_with_block` at the production call shape, varies the budget across four orders of magnitude through the environment variable, once through `[retrieval] token_budget` and once through neither, and requires every lock in the block each time. The TOML arm asserts its own resolution before it measures, so a config file the resolver never discovers fails instead of agreeing with the other arms for the wrong reason. A change that gives this lane a trim fails it. `benchmarks/injection_budget_bytes.py` keeps measuring the lane, at an explicit `SESSION_START_PROBE_BUDGET` literal of its own with the production path's "passes none" stated beside it, and still reports which cap ended each arm (`token_budget` / `l25_subbudget` / `both` / `pool`) — the `session_start` row reads `pool` for both arms at every content length, which is the evidence for this deletion rather than a restatement of it.

  **A user-facing page promised the budget this deletion removes, and it now says what the bound really is.** `docs/user/PRIVACY.md` listed "a token budget for each query … 1,500 for the UserPromptSubmit hook and the SessionStart hook" under *What aelfrice does not control*, as a mitigation limiting what reaches the cloud model. The SessionStart half was never reachable, and this deletion is what severs it from the code, so the page now states the two budgets that are real, says the budget trims the query-relevant lanes rather than the locks, and gives the SessionStart block its actual bound: the lock count, readable with `aelf locked`. `docs/user/CONFIG.md` gains the other half — `[retrieval] token_budget` now reaches that lane, and cannot change it. The page's other promises have guards and this one now does too: `tests/test_privacy_budget_claims_1546.py` holds the two published figures to the shipped constants, the "carries no token budget" sentence to the signature of both frames on the lane, and the deleted constant to its absence. The [#1469](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1469) derived-figure marker is not the mechanism, because it does not work in this file: `scripts/check_derived_figures.py` blanks inline code before parsing markers and pairs backticks across the whole file, and a triple-backtick fence line carries an odd one — `PRIVACY.md` has six fence lines, and two markers planted in it parsed as zero while the gate reported green over prose that contradicted them. Removing the fence lines from a copy makes both parse. That is a hole in the gate, filed as [#1556](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1556) rather than worked around here: three regex repairs on this branch each fixed the previous reading and introduced a new one, so deciding which text is code wants a block scanner and its own acceptance criteria. The page's figures are guarded by the test above until that lands.

  **The budget re-tune this deletion unblocks stays parked, and no budget constant moves.** A byte count is not an acceptable basis for choosing these numbers: the effect of the [#1526](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1526) accounting correction is the ratio of a rendered line to its content, which is largest on the shortest beliefs and shrinks towards parity as they grow, so any constant picked to hold bytes flat holds them flat at one belief length and deviates either side — and on the `Grep|Glob` lane, which truncates at `PER_LINE_CHAR_CAP`, it changes sign. Three things would unblock the re-tune, and all three are required. First, a **gold set with per-turn answer-quality labels**, and an A/B of the current budgets against the proposed ones reported at answer level, not in injected bytes. Second, a **decision rule fixed in writing before the numbers arrive**, so the budget is not fitted to whichever arm won. Third, the **per-lane latency cost**, because raising a budget raises pack work on every hook fire. LongMemEval-S is measured at 98.4% recall with no headroom and may not distinguish the arms at all; if no available corpus can, that is the finding, and the right outcome is to leave the constants where they are and record why.
