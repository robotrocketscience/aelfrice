### Removed

- **`hook.DEFAULT_SESSION_START_TOKEN_BUDGET` and the `token_budget` parameter of `hook.session_start()` are deleted; the SessionStart block is bounded by the lock count ([#1546](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1546)).** The constant could not bind on the lane it governed, at any value. `session_start` retrieves through `_retrieve_baseline_with_block`, which calls `retrieve()` with an **empty query**, and in `retrieve_with_tiers` every relevance lane — L2.5, L1, HRR expansion, the temporal spine and the BFS hop — is gated on `query.strip()`. Only L0 contributes, and L0 is appended unconditionally and never trimmed ([#379](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/379)), so the pack loop a budget drives is never entered. Reproduced before the deletion on a 300-lock store of 150-character beliefs: 300 hits and 61,144 rendered bytes at `token_budget=1`, at 1500 and at 100000 alike. Nothing else could set the value either — no environment variable and no `.aelfrice.toml` key ever reached it, and the only override was a keyword argument no caller passed. `hook._retrieve_baseline_with_block` now passes no budget at all, which leaves `AELFRICE_RETRIEVAL_TOKEN_BUDGET` as the one knob that reaches the lane, and the block is identical at every value of that too. The bound that remains is the one [#379](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/379) states: every locked belief is injected, and the lock count is the operator's knob.

  The guard is kept and re-aimed. `tests/test_render_cost_1526.py::test_session_start_lane_never_trims_its_l0_pool` (renamed from `test_session_start_budget_does_not_bind_on_its_own_lane`) asserts the [#379](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/379) contract directly rather than asserting a now-deleted constant: it drives the real `_retrieve_baseline_with_block` at the production call shape, varies the budget across four orders of magnitude through the environment variable plus an unset arm, and requires every lock in the block each time. A change that gives this lane a trim fails it. `benchmarks/injection_budget_bytes.py` keeps measuring the lane, at an explicit `SESSION_START_PROBE_BUDGET` literal of its own with the production path's "passes none" stated beside it, and still reports which cap ended each arm (`token_budget` / `l25_subbudget` / `both` / `pool`) — the `session_start` row reads `pool` for both arms at every content length, which is the evidence for this deletion rather than a restatement of it.

  **The budget re-tune this deletion unblocks stays parked, and no budget constant moves.** A byte count is not an acceptable basis for choosing these numbers: the effect of the [#1526](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1526) accounting correction is the ratio of a rendered line to its content, which is largest on the shortest beliefs and shrinks towards parity as they grow, so any constant picked to hold bytes flat holds them flat at one belief length and deviates either side — and on the `Grep|Glob` lane, which truncates at `PER_LINE_CHAR_CAP`, it changes sign. Three things would unblock the re-tune, and all three are required. First, a **gold set with per-turn answer-quality labels**, and an A/B of the current budgets against the proposed ones reported at answer level, not in injected bytes. Second, a **decision rule fixed in writing before the numbers arrive**, so the budget is not fitted to whichever arm won. Third, the **per-lane latency cost**, because raising a budget raises pack work on every hook fire. LongMemEval-S is measured at 98.4% recall with no headroom and may not distinguish the arms at all; if no available corpus can, that is the finding, and the right outcome is to leave the constants where they are and record why.
