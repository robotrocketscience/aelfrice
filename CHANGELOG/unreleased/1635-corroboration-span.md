### Fixed

- **A burst of re-assertions no longer counts as independent corroboration ([#1635](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1635)).** Every consumer of a belief's corroboration count read "at least N rows across at least 2 sessions" as independent evidence. A scripted replay gets a new session id every few seconds, so one afternoon of headless evaluation runs gave 67 beliefs 13 or 27 rows each, all inside about 1.5 minutes. All 67 were born inside that burst, and all 67 reached `aelf core`, and through it the SessionStart `<core>` lane. A belief's sightings are now its creation and its corroboration rows, grouped into episodes. Sightings less than `CORROBORATION_EPISODE_GAP_SECONDS` (one hour of elapsed time) apart are one episode, however many sessions they name. The corroboration arm counts only at `CORROBORATION_MIN_EPISODES` (two) or more. The rule applies in `aelf core` (its gate and its `corroboration` / `CORR=` labels), the `<core>` lane, `find_promotable_phantoms`, and `find_promotable_snapshots`. All four read one SQL definition, through `MemoryStore.corroboration_episodes`. Sightings are compared as instants, not as text, because rows mix `Z` and `+HH:MM` offsets. An unparseable timestamp is not a sighting. Each corroboration row carries the turn's own time, so a bulk backfill of old sessions keeps its real spread. A belief said on one day and re-asserted in a single sitting days later has two episodes and counts. A belief that already existed and is then hit by one burst also has two, and also counts; the operator accepted that case over a rule that would drop the first. The posterior arm of `aelf core` is unchanged. On a working store measured read-only, core membership is unchanged at 698.
