### Fixed

- **A burst of re-assertions no longer counts as independent corroboration ([#1635](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1635)).** `aelf core` and the SessionStart `<core>` lane read a corroboration count of 2 or more as independent evidence, and the two promotion selectors read at least N rows across at least 2 sessions the same way. A scripted replay gets a new session id every few seconds, so one afternoon of headless evaluation runs gave 67 beliefs 13 or 27 rows each, all inside about 1.5 minutes. All 67 reached `aelf core`, which made them eligible for the SessionStart `<core>` lane. A belief's sightings are now its creation and its corroboration rows, grouped into episodes. Sightings less than `CORROBORATION_EPISODE_GAP_SECONDS` (one hour of elapsed time, measured to the millisecond) apart are one episode, however many sessions they name. The corroboration arm counts only at `CORROBORATION_MIN_EPISODES` (two) or more. The rule applies in `aelf core` (its gate and its `corroboration` / `CORR=` labels), the `<core>` lane, `find_promotable_phantoms`, and `find_promotable_snapshots`. All four use one SQL definition: the selectors embed it, and `aelf core` and the `<core>` lane read it through `MemoryStore.corroboration_episodes`. Sightings are compared as instants, not as text, because rows mix `Z` and `+HH:MM` offsets. An unparseable timestamp is not a sighting. Transcript ingest stamps each corroboration row with the turn's own time, so a bulk backfill of old sessions keeps its real spread. A belief said on one day and re-asserted in a single sitting days later has two episodes and counts. A belief that already existed and is then hit by one burst also has two, and also counts; the operator accepted that case over a rule that would drop the first. The posterior arm of `aelf core` is unchanged. On a working store that holds no burst beliefs, measured read-only on 2026-09-29, non-lock core membership is unchanged at 698: the rule cost no real recurrence there. This change was not re-measured on the store that held the burst. The `<core>` lane's `corr=` attribute still shows the raw count.
