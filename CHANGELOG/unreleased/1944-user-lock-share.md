### Changed

- **User locks get a share of each prompt instead of all of it ([#1944](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1944)).** User-scope locks reach every repository, and on a store of 70 of them they filled the prompt hook's output: 98.5% of the injected rows were locks, and the median block sat at the 9,500-character limit. Together, user locks now claim at most `[retrieval] user_lock_share` of the prompt hook's output room and of the retrieval token budget, 0.35 by default. A user lock past the share appears only in room that the repository's locks and the retrieval hits leave empty, and the block's last line names each one it can't show. The default comes from copies of 24 repository stores: their own locks took at most 3,005 rendered characters in 23 of them, which with the 3,000-character relevance floor leaves 36.8% of the room. To change it, set `AELFRICE_USER_LOCK_SHARE` or `[retrieval] user_lock_share` to a number from 0 to 1; `1` restores the old behavior. `aelf doctor` now warns when the user store holds more locks than the share can show. The share doesn't apply to the `SessionStart` block, which holds only locks, or to the search-tool and rebuild blocks, which keep their own bounds.
