### Reverted

- **`query_strategy` default returns to `legacy-bm25` ([#1501](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1501)).** The `stack-r1-r3` stack did not get worse; the baseline got better and overtook it. [#1177](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1177) replaced the conjunctive FTS5 MATCH with a disjunction over the rarest tokens, which is the recall cliff R3 existed to work around. Measured across that one commit on the same 30-row labelled corpus that justified the [#718](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/718) flip: `legacy-bm25` 0.3006 → 0.9553, `stack-r1-r3` 0.5858 → 0.8229, uplift +0.2851 → −0.1324. With no AND-set left to widen, the R3 clip only removes candidates — on a 16,454-belief store it drops 1,284 of the 1,859 terms that reach it (69.1%), and its boost half cannot compensate because it cannot fire: a term is boosted only when its IDF is strictly above the 75th percentile, and on that store the 75th percentile and the maximum IDF are the same value. `stack-r1-r3` stays selectable via `[rebuilder] query_strategy`, and both R-modules stay under test. Affects the context-rebuilder path (PreCompact / SessionStart), the transform's only caller. `tests/bench_gate/test_query_strategy.py` now asserts that the shipped default is the winning arm on the corpus, so it goes red on a re-flip and on a regression of the disjunctive MATCH; it stamps the corpus digest into the failure text. Found by the bench-gate tier at the v4.3.0 cut, its first run under [#1477](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1477).
