### Added

- **Core candidates are labeled in the background, with nothing added to your conversation ([#1741](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1741)).** When the Stop hook finds the session's new core candidates and the `claude` CLI is on your `PATH`, it records a batch of up to 50 and starts a detached process. That process runs `claude -p --model haiku` with your existing login, with no tools, MCP servers, skills, CLAUDE.md, auto memory, or hooks, and with a $0.10 cap per run. It then caches the labels the way `aelf core-gate accept` does. The Stop isn't slowed down, and the classifier version doesn't change.
  - **Measured:** on a 519-item reference set, the labels agree with the two-rater reference 83.2% of the time, against 83.0% for the earlier smallest-tier labeling. A 50-belief batch costs about $0.04.
  - **What leaves the machine:** the text of the batched beliefs, to the model behind your Claude Code login. [Privacy](../docs/user/PRIVACY.md) lists it as the third outbound path that's on by default.
  - **To turn it off:** set `[core_gate] background = false` or `AELFRICE_CORE_GATE_BACKGROUND=0`. With it off, or without `claude` on your `PATH`, the #1740 opt-in turn applies if you turned it on. It doesn't run in headless sessions or on the Codex host.
  - **Diagnostics:** each run appends one line, with no belief text, to `core_gate_background.jsonl` beside the store. A run that fails leaves its batch open, and `aelf doctor core-gate --emit` batches those beliefs again.
