### CI

- **The mutation report's mutant total now counts method mutants, and its status counts add up to that total ([#1709](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1709)).** mutmut 3 names a method's mutants with `ǁ` separators, for example `pkg.mod.xǁBoxǁshrink__mutmut_1`. The pattern that counted mutants allowed only letters, digits, `_`, and `.` in the key, so both the weekly job and the per-PR job left every method mutant out of the total, while the killed and survived counts included them. On a report with one function mutant and two method mutants, GNU grep 3.11 in `ubuntu:24.04` counted a total of 1 against 3 killed, under both `LC_ALL=C` and `LC_ALL=C.UTF-8`. The pattern now accepts any key without a space or a colon, which counts all 3 under both locales. The per-PR report also lists `segfault` and `check was interrupted by user`, which completes mutmut 3.8.0's list of statuses, and adds an `unlisted status` line whenever the listed counts don't add up to the total. `tests/test_mutation_report_count_1709.py` runs both steps with bash and the system grep against a fixed report.
