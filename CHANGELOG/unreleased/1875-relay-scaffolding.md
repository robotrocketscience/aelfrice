### Fixed

- **Relay text that opens a user record is no longer stored as your words ([#1875](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/pull/1875)).** The host writes a teammate's brief, a background agent's report, another session's message, and its own cross-session notices as user records. When one of these opens the record, the transcript logger and ingest now cut it and keep what follows, including a relay block later in the record. No cut is made when ingest would keep less of the rest than it keeps from the whole record, and a cut can keep more, such as words after a harness tag inside the relay that never closes. A relay tag at the start that never closes takes the record up to the first paste, words you typed there included, and pastes stay as they were. The turn key of a record that opens with relay text changes, so `aelf doctor --retire-scheduled-prompts` no longer finds a scheduled turn of that shape that was ingested before you upgraded, and keeps the beliefs it made. WebSearch and WebFetch queries to memory now drop function words and repeated terms before the five-term cut, and Grep and Glob patterns drop only repeats.
