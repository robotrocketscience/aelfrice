### Fixed

- **Relay text that opens a user record is no longer stored as your words ([#1875](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/pull/1875)).** The host writes a teammate's brief, a background agent's report, another session's message, and its own cross-session notices as user records. When one of these opens the record, the transcript logger and ingest now cut it and keep what follows. Everything from your first word on stays, including a relay block later in the record, so a relay cut can't take words you typed after it. An unclosed relay tag at the start takes the record up to the first paste, and pastes stay as they were. WebSearch and WebFetch queries to memory now drop function words and repeated terms before the five-term cut, and Grep and Glob patterns drop only repeats.
