### Removed

- **The unwired lock-consistency library is gone ([#1658](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1658)).** `src/aelfrice/lock_consistency.py` and its 24 tests are deleted. The module was the library half of [#1175](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1175) and was never wired into retrieval or the prompt hook. [#1365](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1365) retired the design on 2026-08-09, after a live sample found that 0 of 42 flagged belief-and-lock pairs were real conflicts. Its suppression rules filter numeric slots only, while enum slots drive 74% of the flags, and nothing binds a slot to its subject. Nothing in aelfrice imported the module, so no command, hook, or retrieval result changes. Reviving the idea needs subject binding and enum suppression, which this module did not provide; its design and measurements stay in git history and in the #1175 entry of `CHANGELOG/v4.md`.
