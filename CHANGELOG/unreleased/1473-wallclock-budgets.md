### Fixed

- **A default-run wall-clock budget that failed from machine load rather than from a regression ([#1473](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1473)).** Re-measured before any work, because the issue's evidence predates [#1472](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1472): an AST census finds 16 clock-asserting tests, 10 of them running by default, and under sustained load on 11 cores 3 of 5 runs failed — every failure on the same one test, while the other nine held. That is a much narrower population than the "seven gate runs lost to seven different tests" the issue describes, because #1472 removed the dominant mechanism. `test_dotdir_plan_scales_linearly` becomes `test_dotdir_plan_dedupes_without_rescanning` and counts path comparisons instead of timing them. Its old assertion was already a ratio rather than a budget, taking `min` of three samples on the reasoning that load can only make a sample slower — true of a single measurement and false of a ratio of two, because under contention the larger case's floor inflates more than the smaller one's. Restoring the #1202 list rescan makes 1,999,000 comparisons for 2,000 strays against 0 for the shipped set-based dedup, three orders of magnitude clear of the bound and identical on an idle or a loaded machine; re-run at load average 69, the converted test passed 5 of 5. `tests/test_wallclock_budget_census_1473.py` lands the census itself as a test, capping the population at its measured members so a new budget cannot be added silently, and failing in both directions — a new default-run budget, and a stale entry left behind after a conversion, which would otherwise hide the next real addition. `hook_latency_ms`'s `assert elapsed >= 0.0` is dropped as vacuous (a duration is non-negative by construction, so it sat in the census by shape while asserting nothing); the test is kept and renamed, because its other assertion — that a future stamp floors to exactly 0.0 — is the branch that would return a negative if the floor were removed, and is load-immune.
