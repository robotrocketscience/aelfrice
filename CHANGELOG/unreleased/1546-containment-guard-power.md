### Fixed

- **The budget census's containment guard had no mutation coverage on three of its six lanes, and its inertness disclosure named the wrong lane ([#1546](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1546)).** `tests/test_budget_census.py` drives the census against a fake packer that returns less at a higher budget, and asserted only that the pooled violation list was non-empty. The fake fired at `token_budget >= 700`, which is below the entire grid of three lanes — `ups` runs 750-3000, `retrieval_default` 1200-4800 and `rebuilder` 2000-8000 — so on those it shrank the probe and every arm by the same item, the comparison stayed consistent, and no violation could arise; the three lanes it could not mutate were carried by the three it could and read as covered. The threshold now sits above every arm and below the probe, and the assertion is per lane. Separately, `NOT_EXERCISED["graph edges"]` claimed the cluster packer was inert on an edgeless store; `pack_with_clusters` in fact runs on every query of every cell with singleton clusters — 2,208 calls in a full run, all of them packing — so the non-monotone fill the module docstring flags is live on every measured cell, K0's included. A new arm pins what the absent edges were hiding: containment is a property of this corpus, not a theorem. With one `TEMPORAL_NEXT` edge an arm reaches a belief the unbudgeted probe did not, because the temporal spine seeds from `l1_packed[:DEFAULT_SPINE_SEED_COUNT]` and `l1_packed` at a low budget is not a prefix of itself at a high one, so a cheap belief lands inside the seed window and its off-lexicon neighbour escapes. Published K0 figures are unchanged — `n=7`, `ec_pp` 0.0 on all six lanes, `violations` 0 — because the committed corpora carry no edges.
