### Documentation

- **`benchmarks/README.md` states the rule that agreement is not quality evidence ([#1453](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1453)).** Top-10 movement rate, Jaccard overlap at k, Kendall tau and "N of M queries changed" are **change** statistics: they say two configurations return different results, not which one is right. A movement rate becomes a quality statistic only against labelled relevance, external to both rankers. Without labels a large movement and a small movement are equally consistent with an improvement, a regression and noise. The note names the two failure modes that have actually occurred — reporting a movement rate as if it were an uplift, and treating agreement between two approximations as validation — and cites [`848dbf83`](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/commit/848dbf83aab6e2c5198b1bc5b0f70ebcc7f11ea9), the revert that retracted a published table for the second reason, after `extracted_query` turned out to be handed to `retrieve()` by neither caller (97.7% of recorded rows come from `user_prompt_submit`, which scores a conversation-aware composition the context rebuilder never sees). It lands in `benchmarks/README.md` rather than in a design document because that is the file a person running one of these harnesses opens. `tests/test_docs_agreement_rule_1453.py` pins that the rule is still stated and that the cited SHA still resolves to a commit — a citation to an unreachable object reads as sourced while pointing at nothing. The note's wording is deliberately not pinned.
