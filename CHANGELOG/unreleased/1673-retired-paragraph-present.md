### Fixed

- **Re-onboarding no longer offers a paragraph whose belief you retired ([#1673](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1673)).** The onboard pre-scan didn't count retired beliefs as present. So every `aelf onboard` run offered a retired paragraph again, the host classified it again, and the accept step dropped it, because capture never revives a retired belief ([#1215](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1215)). `aelf onboard --check` also never reached zero new candidates. Now retired content counts as present: it isn't offered or classified again, and it stays retired.
