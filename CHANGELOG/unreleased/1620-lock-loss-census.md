### Internal

- **The lock-loss figures in [#1620](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1620) now have a producer.** `benchmarks/lock_loss_census_1620.py` re-derives each figure from a store, opened read-only through a `mode=ro` URI: the `lock_level` distribution over active beliefs and over every row, reported separately; beliefs whose content is an aelfrice command; beliefs that hold a rendered `<belief id=` block; and active speculative-origin beliefs. With `--pattern`, it also lists one instruction's family with each member's posterior mean and corroboration count, plus its `lock:unlock` and `lock:expire` history. Stores are per repository, so you can pass `--store` more than once, and the cross-store view shows a lock that landed in another repository's store. Content is never printed beyond 80 characters. `tests/test_lock_loss_census_1620.py` covers each figure on synthetic stores and checks that a run leaves the store and its WAL byte-identical.
