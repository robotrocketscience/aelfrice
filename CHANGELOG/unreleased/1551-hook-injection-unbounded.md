### Fixed

- **The UserPromptSubmit block is bounded again ([#1551](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1551)).** `DEFAULT_HOOK_TOKEN_BUDGET = 1500` is passed to `retrieve()` and the packers in `clustering.py` honour it, but `<locked>`, `<core>`, `<recent-work>` and the retrieval hits are packed independently and concatenated, so no budget bounded the block that reached the model. `_write_hook_audit_record` already measured the result with the same 4-chars-per-token estimator the budgeter uses and wrote the overrun to `hook_audit.jsonl`; nothing acted on it. Two bounds close the gap. `_cap_belief_content` truncates one belief's content at `BELIEF_CONTENT_CHAR_CAP = 1200` when the `<belief>` element is rendered, the equivalent of `hook_search_tool.PER_LINE_CHAR_CAP` which had no UserPromptSubmit counterpart; and `enforce_block_ceiling` drops whole `<belief>` elements from the end of the assembled body until it fits `HOOK_BLOCK_TOKEN_CEILING`, four times the per-lane budget so it never fires on a healthy store, overridable with `AELFRICE_HOOK_BLOCK_CEILING` and disabled by `0`. Only complete elements are removed, so the framing sections and the `<aelfrice-locks-manifest>` are never touched and the block stays well-formed. The per-belief cap is a guard against a pathological row rather than a retrieval-quality knob: whether `1500` is still the right amount of context under #1526's corrected accounting is the open question that constant's docstring already flags, and re-tuning it needs a retrieval-quality gate that this change does not attempt. A capped belief keeps its id, so its full text stays reachable through `aelf search` or an anchored document, and `--reference` locks remain the intended home for long-form material.
