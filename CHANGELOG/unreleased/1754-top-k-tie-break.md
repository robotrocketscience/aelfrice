### Fixed

- **The HRR structural probe and the spectral lane's BM25 seeds keep the lowest belief ids when more candidates tie than they keep ([#1754](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1754)).** Both selected their top `k` with `argpartition`, which keeps an arbitrary subset of the candidates tied at the cutoff. The subset was the same on every run, but it didn't follow belief id, and a later sort can't restore a candidate that was already dropped. Ties are common in the probe: beliefs with the same outgoing edges have identical rows.
  - **Selection:** the probe and `seeds_from_bm25` now order by score, then by row, and rows follow belief id. With 17 beliefs that each support one target, a probe with `top_k` from 1 to 16 now returns the lowest ids every time. Before, it didn't for most values of `top_k`.
  - **Scores:** a BLAS matrix-vector product can score two identical rows one ulp apart, depending on the row's position and the matrix shape. On Apple Accelerate, 41 identical rows gave 2 distinct scores. The probe now re-scores the rows near its cutoff with one reduction loop for every row, so a tie in the data stays a tie.
  - **Measured:** at 20,000 beliefs and `dim=512`, interleaved in one process, the probe's median cost rose by 73 µs and 93 µs over two runs (+2.4% and +3.5%). `seeds_from_bm25` rose from 66 µs to 100 µs per call.
