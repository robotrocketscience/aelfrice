### Fixed

- **The prior-grid sweep no longer counts mixed-prior dedupe merges as moved posteriors ([#1598](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1598)).** `benchmarks/posterior_prior_grid_sweep.py` separated off-grid beliefs into dedupe mass and genuinely-moved posteriors by testing whether `(α, β)` is an exact integer multiple of one insertion prior, which assumes a duplicate group holds copies of the same prior. `content_hash` is `sha256` of the belief text alone, so beliefs with identical text but a different type or source share a hash and get merged when the store is opened; their summed `(α, β)` is neither on the grid nor a multiple of any single prior, and read as evidence that the feedback loop ran. It is not — no feedback event occurred. The classification now tests whether a pair is an exact sum of two or more priors and reports three categories instead of two: a uniform merge, whose mean is unchanged and which is therefore rank-neutral; a **mixed merge, whose mean does move but which is still not evidence**; and the remainder. Measured across 26 stores, 97 beliefs were in the middle category and had been counted as evidence. The search uses exact float equality rather than a tolerance, which is sound because summation order does not change the result for this prior set — a property measured over 120,000 random permutations of 16-term sums and pinned by a test, so that if `TYPE_PRIORS` changes and the property lapses the exact match is flagged rather than silently wrong. It is a breadth-first expansion over reachable sums, so its cost is the roughly 103,000 distinct sums rather than the number of combinations, and its group-size bound is stated and enforced by a test. Also fixes a population mismatch in the same producer: the pre-#1086 legacy count was taken over every off-grid belief while being printed as a share of the genuinely-moved ones, which mixed dedupe mass into the numerator and could print a share above 100%.
