### Documentation

- **A proposal for gating `aelf core` admission on content ([#1638](https://github.com/robotrocketscience/aelfrice/issues/1638)).** `docs/feature-core-admission-gate.md` proposes that a belief qualifies for core's non-lock arms only if an offline, cached classifier has judged it truth-apt. It records the measured problem, the expected effect, three precision configurations with their costs, a required self-verification check for silently failed batch runs, and the two questions still open. Nothing is implemented, and no shipped behavior changes.
